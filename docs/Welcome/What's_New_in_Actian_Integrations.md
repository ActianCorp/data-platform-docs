# What’s New in Actian Integrations

The Actian Data Platform introduces Integrations as a Service, which enhances the user experience, streamlines processes, and simplifies terminology, resulting in a more accessible and intuitive platform. The goal is to help prepare for the next wave of emerging technologies, such as Generative AI, by offering the ability to ensure they are gathering complete, high-quality data to train AI models in addition to operational decisions.

Enhancements include:

- A new Integration Workspace, complete with dedicated design and management navigation.
- No-Code Data Transformations

- New Connector options
