## Managing Templates

Templates contain integration settings that are reusable by linked configurations (see [Managing Configurations](../Integrations/Managing_Configurations.md)). Templates include a package, macros, and a location to execute the job.

You can create templates (see [Creating Templates](../Integrations/Creating_Templates.md)), and manage them on the Templates page.
