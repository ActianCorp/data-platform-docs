## Managing Files

You can upload files for use and manage them on the Files page. Files can be public (created by an administrator) or private (created by a user), and reside in a Files page, or under Templates, Configurations or Jobs.

You can also view and manage files associated with a configuration or template.

- To view macros associated with a particular configuration, see [Manage Configuration Files](../Integrations/Manage_Configuration_Files.md).
- To view macros associated with a particular template, see [Manage Template Files](../Integrations/Manage_Template_Files.md).
