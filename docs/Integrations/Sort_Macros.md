## Sort Macros

This topic describes how to sort macros in the Integrations, Manage, Macros page. You can also view and manage macros associated with a configuration or template.

To sort the macros list

1. Click **Integrations, Manage,** **Macros**.

    The **Macros** page displays the available macros.

2. Click the **Macro** or **Value** column header.

    The macros are sorted based on the column header you click.
