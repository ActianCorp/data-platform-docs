## Common Tasks

The following are the most typical workflow tasks for building and maintaining integrations in the Integration console:

- [Create an integration](../Integrations/Creating_Integrations.md)or[import an integration](../Integrations/Import_Integration.md).
- [Verify integration run history](../Integrations/View_Integration_Run_History.md).

- [Manage an integration](../Integrations/Managing_Integrations.md).
- [Manage a configuration](../Integrations/Managing_Configurations.md).

- [Schedule a configuration to execute](../Integrations/Edit_Configuration_Schedule.md).
- [Create a connection](../Integrations/Create_Connections.md), [create a service](../Integrations/Create_a_Service.md), and [create an endpoint](../Integrations/Create_an_Endpoint.md).

- [Add a template](../Integrations/Creating_Templates.md), [add a macro](../Integrations/Creating_Macros.md), [add a file](../Integrations/Managing_Files.md), and [add an agent](../Integrations/Managing_Agents_and_Devices.md).
- [Monitor the health of integrations, configurations and agents](../Integrations/Overview_Page.md).

After you create and run your integration, you may take advantage of these features:

    - [Launch Query Editor](../User/Launch_Query_Editor.md)to query your target data: Navigate to Warehouse, select your warehouse, then select Query Editor.

    - Visualize your target data: Navigate to Warehouse, click on your warehouse, Connections, Connect to Actian Warehouse, then select an application to visualize your data ([Connect to Tableau with ODBC](../User/ConnectTableauODBC.md), [Connect to Looker with JDBC](../User/ConnectLookerJDBC.md), [Connect to Power BI with ODBC](../User/ConnectPowerBIODBC.md), DBVisualizer, or Squirrel SQL).
