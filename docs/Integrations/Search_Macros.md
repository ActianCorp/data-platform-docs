## Search Macros

This topic describes how to search macros in the Integrations, Manage, Macros page. You can also view and manage macros associated with a configuration or template.

To search for public macros

1. Click **Integrations, Manage,** **Macros**.

    The **Macros** page displays the available macros.

2. Click ![](images/SearchIcon.png).
3. Enter the search term.

The macros that match the search term automatically display.
