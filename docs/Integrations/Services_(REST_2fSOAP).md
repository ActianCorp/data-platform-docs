## Services (REST/SOAP)

Service definitions define HTTP, REST or SOAP services. Once created, service definitions can be used as a source type in integrations. Service definitions are comprised of service-level parameters, and a collection of specific endpoints. Aspects of the service and endpoint definition can be parameterized, allowing for consumers of the service to provide different values for these parameters within their integration definitions.

Services can either be added manually, or can be imported from OpenAPI specifications, Postman collections or a WSDL file.
