## Managing Macros

You can upload macros for use with your templates and configurations, and manage them on the Macros page. Macros can be public (created by an administrator) or private (created by a user), and reside in a Macros page, or under Templates, Configurations or Jobs.

You can also view and manage files associated with a configuration or template.

- To view macros associated with a particular configuration, see [Manage Configuration Macros](../Integrations/Manage_Configuration_Macros.md).
- To view macros associated with a particular template, see [Manage Template Macros](../Integrations/Manage_Template_Macros.md).
