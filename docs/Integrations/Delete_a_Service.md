## Delete a Service

To delete a service

1. On the Integrations Console, select Design.
2. Select Services (REST/SOAP).

3. Select single or multiple check boxes from the list to indicate those services that you want to delete.
A toolbar opens with Delete ![](images/DeleteIcon.png).

4. Click Delete ![](images/DeleteIcon.png). Or click Information ![](images/Information%20Icon.png) to delete the service.

    A confirmation pop-up opens to prompt you for deletion confirmation.

5. Click Yes, delete to delete the desired service.

    A confirmation message displays: “Selected Service has been deleted.”
