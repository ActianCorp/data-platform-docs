## Check a Service

To check a service

On the Monitoring console page (see [Display the Warehouse Monitoring Console](../User/Display_the_Warehouse_Monitoring_Console.md)), click the name of the service you want to check.

The Service page is displayed, listing the service information (see [Warehouse Monitoring Services](../User/Warehouse_Monitoring_Services.md)).

To graph the data, click the Graph link (see [Graphing Service Information](../User/Graphing_Service_Information.md)).
