## Zoom in on Graphing Data

You can zoom in on data displayed on a service graph using the following methods:

- The Zoom tool:

![](images/ZoomToolIcon.png)

    Click the icon and then click and drag the mouse across to select the time span you want to zoom in on.

- The Zoom in tool:

![](images/ZoomInToolIcon.png)

    Click the icon to continue narrowing the time span on the graph.

- The Zoom out tool:

![](images/ZoomOutToolIcon.png)

    Click the icon to expand the time span on the graph.

- The timeline sliders below the graph:

![](images/TimelineSliders.png)

    Move each slider to narrow or expand the time span on the graph.

- The Autoscale tool:

![](images/AutoscaleToolIcon.png)

    Click the icon to return the chart display to its original scale.
