## SOC2 Security Attestation Available

SSAE 18 SOC2, developed by the American Institute of CPAs, is an auditing procedure that ensures service providers securely manage your data to protect your organization’s interests and your clients’ privacy. The procedure results in a document used by companies and audit firms to confirm that the controls provided in Actian’s services meet your control standards. You would use this document for guidance in setting up your security and processing controls.

The Actian Data Platform can provide you with a watermarked SOC2 Type II attestation document, available upon request with a signed non-disclosure agreement. For more information, contact sales@actian.com.

For more information about SSAE 18, SOC2, and related standards, see [SSAE 16, SSAE18, SOC 1, SOC2: What they are and why you should care](https://www.atlantic.net/hipaa-compliant-hosting/ssae-16-soc-1-soc2-care/).
