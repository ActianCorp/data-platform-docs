## Contact Support

Administrators may contact Actian Support.

To contact Support

1. Open the Administration interface (see [Access the Administration Interface](../DBaaS_User/Database_Administration.md)).
2. On the menu pane on the left side of the window, click Contact Support.

    A new browser window opens, where you may create a support case.
