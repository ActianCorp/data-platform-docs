## Using the Assistant

The onboarding survey you completed the first time you logged in to the Actian Data Platform stores your answers to these questions:

- Role – tasks you are typically responsible for
- Criteria – cloud criteria you want to evaluate

- Current Solutions – which data platform(s) you currently use
- Analytics Solutions – data analytics platforms you will connect to

- Data Size – size of dataset you will use

Your answers tailor the Assistant to best help you with the tasks you need to successfully get started with the Actian Data Platform.

To open the Assistant

Click this icon to the right of your username in the header bar:

![](images/Avalanche_Assistant_icon.png)

The Assistant opens a pane on the right side of the web console. It contains a menu bar on the left side with these options:

**Resources**

:   Contains links to Actian Academy videos, Community articles, and documentation topics.

**Recommended Tasks**

:   Displays beginning tasks based on your onboarding survey answers. The tasks list is divided into tasks not tried yet and those you have completed.

**Actian Community**

:   Displays Actian Community activities:

    - Recommended articles from the Actian Community

    - My Questions you will post or have posted to gather responses from the support team and other Actian users

    - Followed displays conversations you are following and have responded to

:   To open the Actian Community page in a new browser window, click this icon: ![](images/Actian_Community_icon.png)

**What’s New**

:   Opens the Integrations dashboard with the “News & Announcements” RSS feel from the Actian Corporation blog (actian.com/feed).

To search the contents of the assistant, click the search icon in the assistant’s title bar.
